AI Week Day 1: Planning (Grafana AI Week — Monday)

2026-Jul-27
ai , observability , english , video , short , grafana labs , grafana ai week , grafana assistant

It’s day one of Grafana Labs’ AI Week, and Monday is all about planning — getting the clarity you need on what’s going on in your system so you can figure out what to build. Kicking things off is Workspace for Grafana Assistant: a full-page view of Assistant so your conversations and generated graphs aren’t crammed into the little right-hand sidebar. It’s a much less cluttered place to run investigations and to be agent-first. On top of that, Assistant is now easier to try — enable it in Grafana 13 for OSS without linking a Grafana Cloud account, or try it with no signup or download at all on Grafana Play.

Transcript

Hi everyone! I’m Nicole van der Hoeven and I’m really excited to welcome you to AI Week. Today is Monday, which is planning day, where we give you the tools you need to get clarity on what even is going on in your system so that you can figure out what to build.

Now we’re kicking things off with a feature called Workspace for Grafana Assistant. Now if you’ve used Assistant before, you’ll know that it comes up on the little sidebar on the right side of Grafana. Well, that can start to get cramped if you have multiple conversations or if you’ve asked Assistant to generate multiple graphs for you. Well, we’ve heard you, and now we’re giving you a full page view of Assistant. So you’ll have all of your conversations on the left, and this is also an excellent place to run investigations from. You can also zoom in on any of those graphs, and it’s just a much less cluttered view if you want to be agent-first.

Speaking of Assistant, the second thing I want to tell you is that you know, Assistant was already available everywhere. But now, Assistant is available… in more places than everywhere? And also more easily. So Assistant was already available for OSS, but you had to link your Grafana Cloud account, which was not so handy if you wanted to try it out. But not anymore. Now if you download Grafana 13 and enable Assistant, then you’ll just be able to go and try it out and chat with Assistant without doing anything else. It’s still not free or open source, but it IS a lot easier to try it out.

And if you don’t want to download anything, that’s what Grafana Play is for. Grafana Assistant is now available to try out on Grafana Play without any signup or any download required. Try it out at play.grafana.org. I really love seeing it there. Tune in tomorrow where we’re going to talk about everything we’re announcing to help you start to build.
