Transcript

It’s the final day of Grafana Labs’ AI Week, and Friday is all about collaborating — using AI right where your team already works, no context switching required.

Today, Grafana Assistant in Slack is generally available. Investigate issues, ask follow-up questions, query your metrics and logs — all from the channel where your incident conversation is already happening.

And that’s just one place Assistant now meets you. Today we also announced: the Grafana mobile app is GA, with Assistant on mobile in public preview. Assistant Desktop is in public preview. Microsoft Teams support is in private preview. And Assistant Search — deeper, context-aware search — is in public preview too.

Add it all up, and Assistant is now wherever you are: the Grafana UI, the API, your terminal, your coding agent, Slack, Teams, mobile, desktop, and search.

That’s a wrap on five days of announcements — and I know it was a lot. So if you missed a day, or you just want the full picture of where AI at Grafana stands today, I made a video for exactly that: everything you can do with AI in Grafana Cloud, including everything we shipped this week. It’s linked below and pinned in the comments.

One last time: grafana.ai for all of it. Thanks for spending AI Week with me.
